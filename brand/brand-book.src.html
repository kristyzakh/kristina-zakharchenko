The one-line brief

Everything in this book exists to make one impression repeatable: a marketer who shows you the numbers. The design is confident and warm; the data is never decorated. Pink carries the personality, ink carries the credibility, and the numbers are always allowed to be the loudest thing on the page.

Who it speaks to

E-commerce founders and marketing leads spending $15K–$100K+/month who want a person, not an agency layer. They read fast, they check numbers, and they have been oversold before.

What it must feel like

Organised, current, human. The visual energy of a good creative studio; the discipline of a media buyer's spreadsheet. Never corporate-blue, never hype.

The test for any surface

Cover the logo. Can a client still tell it's yours from the type, the pink and the way numbers are set? If not, the surface isn't finished.

01

Logo

Two marks, one system. The primary logo is KZ. — your initials and a full stop, redrawn from the site header in the brand's own display face. The full stop is the whole idea: it's the punctuation of someone who finishes the sentence, and it carries the accent colour so the brand shows up even at 20 px. Where the dot can't survive — avatars, favicons, square stamps — a compact ligature takes over.

Primary mark

Ink + magenta dot · primary
Cream + bubblegum dot
One colour · on bubblegum
One colour · magenta

The dot changes colour, the letters don’t. On light grounds the dot is magenta; on ink and on imagery it is bubblegum. Everywhere the logo must be a single colour — a fax-grade PDF, an engraved stamp, a client’s partner wall — use the one-colour version and let the dot go with the letters. Never the other way around: ink letters with a yellow dot, or pink letters with an ink dot, are both off-brand.

Spacing is fixed. The gap between Z and the full stop is set in the files at 16% of the cap height. It is wider than a typographic full stop on purpose — the dot has to read as a separate element, not as punctuation that got stuck to the Z. Don’t retype the logo; use the file.

Construction

xx

Clear space. Keep a margin of x — half the height of the monogram — on all four sides. Nothing enters it: no text, no rules, no photo edges, no other logos.

Minimum sizes — and which mark to use

KZ. holds down to about 20 px; below that the dot starts to disappear and you switch to the ligature. The ligature itself closes into a blob below 24 px, so anything smaller — favicons, app icons, table headers, under 6 mm in print — takes the small-size cut: same letters, positive spacing.

Where a full stop would be lost or a square needs filling, the K and Z are tracked until the K's leg and the Z's shoulder share one diagonal. It reads as a single shape at a glance and as two letters on the second look — right for avatars, app tiles, stamps and anything set very small. Six approved colourways; on photography it is always cream, and always in an area dark and quiet enough to hold it. If it isn't, darken the area rather than outlining the mark.

The overprint version

The K in bubblegum, the Z in sun, and the intersection in magenta — as if two inks were printed over each other. It's the brand's one piece of theatre: covers, launch posts, the first slide. Never inside a report, never below 60 px, never re-coloured. The overlap is a real filled shape in the file, not a blend mode, so it survives PDF export and print.

Horizontal is the working lockup: document headers, letterheads, deck footers, invoices, email signature. Vertical is for square and centred spaces: title slides, tote-bag logic, a proposal cover. Never rebuild either by hand — the spacing between mark, rule and name is fixed in the files.

The name is set in Unbounded SemiBold, the role line in Inter Tight Medium with wide tracking. In Ukrainian the same lockup takes «КРИСТІНА ЗАХАРЧЕНКО» — set it from the file provided, don't retype it in a different face.

Two tiers. The plain lockup carries the discipline only — DIGITAL & PERFORMANCE MARKETING — and is the default for page furniture. The focus version adds E-COMMERCE · LEAD GENERATION underneath, for the surfaces where a reader is deciding whether you're for them: proposal covers, the LinkedIn banner, a deck's title slide, a conference badge. Never both tiers on the same page.

Wordmark

Stacked wordmark, no mark. Use where the monogram would be redundant — an About page header, a CV masthead, the back of a card.

Signature line

The line from the site footer, kept as an official asset: name in Inter Tight SemiBold, role in Medium at 62% opacity, joined by an em dash. It is the smallest complete statement of the brand — use it in email footers, PDF page furniture and document properties.

NoRotated or tilted

Also off-limits: re-typesetting KZ in another font, adding a tagline inside the mark, outlining it, boxing it in a colour outside the palette, or putting the overprint version on any background other than paper or ink. And three rules for the dot specifically — never drop it from the primary mark, never make it yellow or lilac, and never make it a different shape (a square, a heart, an asterisk). If the dot doesn't fit, that's the signal to switch to the ligature.

Typography

Four roles, four faces, and a checked answer for both alphabets. Every face here has been verified to contain the full Ukrainian set — ґ Ґ, є Є, і І, ї Ї and the hryvnia sign ₴ — so a Ukrainian document never silently falls back to Times New Roman halfway through a headline.

Why headings swap language. Inter Tight is drawn tight and narrow — excellent for English, but Ukrainian runs 10–15% longer and its Cyrillic gets cramped at heading weight. Golos Text is a Cyrillic-first grotesque with the same neo-grotesque skeleton, so a Ukrainian deck reads as the same brand without looking squeezed. Title, body and data stay identical across both languages, so 80% of the system never changes.

Typographic house rules

  • ✓Sentence case for headings. Never Title Case — it reads corporate.
  • ✓Numerals always, including one to nine. 3 markets, not three markets.
  • ✓Tabular figures in every table, KPI and chart axis, so columns line up.
  • ✓One display line per surface. Two competing big lines is the most common mistake.
  • ✗No italics for emphasis — use weight or colour. Unbounded has no true italic.
  • ✗No letter-spacing on lowercase body text. Only uppercase labels get tracking.

05

Tone of voice

The voice in one line: the operator who shows you the numbers. Warm enough to work with, specific enough to trust. It is one person speaking — never an agency "we", never a guru.

1 · Show the number, not the adjective

"Great results" means nothing. "ROAS 3.4 → 4.8 in 11 weeks" means something. If a sentence has no number in it, it should be doing something else — explaining, deciding or admitting.

2 · Action first, effect second

Say what you did, then what it did. "I cut the two overlapping audiences, and CPA fell 20%." An effect with no action behind it reads as luck.

3 · Be honest early

Name the trade-off, the risk or the month that didn't work before anyone asks. Nothing buys more trust with a founder who has been oversold before.

Where the voice sits

FormalCasual
SeriousPlayful
Matter-of-factEnthusiastic
ExpertPeer

Casual but precise. Dry humour is allowed; jokes are not. Enthusiasm is expressed by how specific you are, not by exclamation marks — the brand uses roughly one per quarter.

Words

Use

spend · ROAS · CPA · AOV · contribution margin · test · cut · scale · hold · the honest read · here's what I'd do · what this means for you · week 3 · flat · paying customers

Avoid

crushing it · 10x · game-changer · secret sauce · guru · ninja · synergy · leverage (as a verb) · revolutionary · just · simply · unlock · deep dive · circle back · 🚀

Two words to be careful with: "growth" is fine as a noun, tired as an adjective. "Optimise" is honest when you name what you optimised, empty when you don't.

Ukrainian-specific rules

  • 01Address clients as ви (lowercase) — respectful without being stiff. Ти only in DMs with people you already work with.
  • 02Speak as я, not ми. It's one person; the singular is the whole point of the brand.
  • 03Keep metric abbreviations in Latin — ROAS, CPA, CTR, AOV, LTV — and translate the sentence around them.
  • 04Decimal comma, space for thousands: 3,4 and 84 120. Hryvnia goes after the number with a non-breaking space.
  • 05Use «lapky» for quotes and the curly apostrophe ’ — straight quotes look like a machine translation.
  • 06Leave 15% more room in Ukrainian headlines. If a translated title breaks to three lines, shorten the sentence rather than the type size.

How to combine it

The five decisions that turn the pieces into a layout. Make them in this order and almost anything comes out on-brand; skip one and the surface starts to look like a template.

#DecisionThe rule
1Pick the groundPaper for anything to be read (reports, proposals, docs). Ink for anything to be looked at (covers, title slides, banners, dashboards). Bubblegum only for short announcements. Never a gradient.
2One display lineExactly one thing in Unbounded per surface — a headline or a number, never both. Everything else steps down to Inter Tight or Inter.
3One pink elementOne fill, one tile, one highlight — the thing you want read first. If two things are pink, neither is emphasised.
4Yellow last, or not at allButter appears at most once, as a small block or a highlight behind text. If pink is already carrying the layout, skip yellow entirely.
5Mono the dataEvery label, unit, axis and eyebrow in Plex Mono uppercase. This is the detail that makes the work read as a marketer's, not a designer's.

Ship checklist — run this before anything leaves your desk

  • 01Is there exactly one display line and exactly one pink element?
  • 02Are all labels and units in Plex Mono uppercase, and all figures tabular?
  • 03Does every number have a comparison next to it — target, last month, or last year?

Set up Google Workspace once

In Docs or Slides: Fonts → More fonts → add Unbounded, Inter Tight, Inter, Golos Text, IBM Plex Mono. Then build one template doc and one template deck with the styles already applied, and start every client file from those instead of a blank page.

Set up Canva once

Brand Kit → upload the five colours by hex, upload the logo SVGs, and upload the font files. Canva doesn't carry Unbounded by default — if the upload isn't available on your plan, use Golos Text 800 for display and note the substitution.

Bring the website in line

The current site uses Fraunces for display, which has no Cyrillic at all — a Ukrainian version would silently fall back to a system serif. Swapping the display face to Unbounded and the greens to this palette makes the site match everything in this book.
