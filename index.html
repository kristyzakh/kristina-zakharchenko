Beauty · Local · Full system

Studio 56 — Beauty studio

Fractional Growth Marketer · Ongoing

Situation

A beauty studio with no marketing system and a small budget, in a niche where quality leads are hard to get.

Approach

Built everything from zero: the strategy, Meta and Google Ads, content, the CRM and the way leads are handled, so every part of the chain supports the next one.

Outcome

August 2026 became the studio's best sales month, and the new autumn strategy brought in its first-ever online booking.

D2C · Shopify · Apparel

D2C Apparel Brand — US / Canada

Performance Marketing Manager (Contract) · Jan 2023 – Present

Situation

A multi-store Shopify ecosystem (production & retail) needed ROAS stability across seasonal peaks, with budgets ranging $15K–$100K+/month and no reliable forecasting.

Approach

Ran Meta Ads across ~18 stores and Google Ads across ~10, built Shopping/PMax segmentation and Merchant Center feed health, and stood up forecasting/pacing models based on seasonality — plus daily reporting with gap detection and action plans.

Outcome

Stabilized ROAS through seasonal peaks, reduced under-spend risk via forecasting, shipped AI-assisted reporting dashboards for real-time visibility, and supported a +25% YoY net revenue increase in the 2026 peak season.

Lead quality · Strategy · Travel

Rebirth Travel — Travel & Tourism

Marketing Lead (Project-Based) · Jun 2025 – Nov 2025

Situation

A travel agency moving upmarket was attracting high volumes of discount-driven enquiries. They needed paid acquisition that brought in fewer, better-qualified leads instead of more cheap ones.

Approach

Built the performance marketing strategy and ran Meta and Google Ads against lead quality rather than volume — with audience segmentation, remarketing, and a lead qualification step before consultation. Set up the reporting and measurement framework to track which campaigns produced real bookings.

Outcome

Shifted acquisition toward higher-value customers, replaced volume-based lead targets with a qualification-based funnel, and left the client with segmentation, remarketing and reporting foundations they could keep scaling.

Lead gen · Fintech · LATAM

White Pony — Fintech / Lead Generation

Performance Marketing Specialist · Jun 2022 – Oct 2023

Situation

A LATAM lead-gen company generating leads for forex/CFD brokers (XM, OffersFX and others) needed higher lead volume at a lower cost per lead, on a ~$20K/month budget.

Approach

Built and managed Meta Ads lead-gen campaigns (image, video and Meta Lead Ads), constantly testing funnels, demographic and interest audiences, and Spanish-language creative to increase lead volume and decrease CPL.

Outcome

Grew qualified lead volume while bringing CPL down through continuous funnel, audience and creative testing. A welcome-flow redesign for the same leads went on to lift broker contactability by 10–15% over baseline.

Also in my background

Organic and community growth for a UK health brand (Canny Dentists), and email lifecycle work — scaling Dosis Diaria past 100,000 subscribers on Beehiiv, and building segmented nurture flows in Mailchimp. Not what I take on day to day now, but it means I read the whole funnel, not just the ad account.
